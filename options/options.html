Policy in Human Terms — Settings

Bring your own OpenAI API key. All analysis happens locally in your browser; the policy text is sent directly to OpenAI using your key.

Disclosure: When you click Analyze, the extension reads the policy text from the current page (or fetches the policy URL you pick) and sends that text to api.openai.com using your API key. The grading rubric and red-flag regexes run locally in your browser. Your API key is stored in chrome.storage.local and never leaves your machine except as the Authorization header to OpenAI.

Cached analyses

Keyed by SHA-256 of the policy text. Clears on extension uninstall.
